# Keyword map — NHR Solution

Target keywords assigned to pages, and the titles and descriptions built from
them. 82 keywords across 10 pages.

**On `<meta name="keywords">`:** not used, deliberately. Google has ignored it
since 2009, Bing treats a stuffed one as a spam signal, and it publishes your
keyword strategy to competitors. Keywords earn rankings by appearing in the
title, the H1, the body copy and the internal link text — which is how they are
applied below.

**Positioning.** Every head term here ("HR software", "payroll software") is
dominated by incumbents with years of domain authority. The realistic wins are
the two columns further right: UK-qualified terms, and the calculator long-tail.
The calculators are the strongest asset on the site for this — they are real
working tools, and tool pages earn links.

## Gaps

**Pages that should exist and do not.** Each is a keyword cluster with no
landing page, and the footer already links to several of them as dead links:

- Construction, retail, startups sector pages (#76–#78)
- Health & safety, eLearning, wellbeing, employment law product pages — the
  homepage sells six product areas but only two have their own page
- A blog or resource centre. The footer links Guides, Articles, Blog and
  Webinars; none exist. This is where the compliance keywords (#57–#64) would
  live, and compliance explainers are what earn links in this market.

**Claims that cap what can be targeted.** Per the legal pages, the product makes
no accreditation claims — not HMRC-recognised, not ISO 27001. So "HMRC
recognised payroll software" and similar cannot be targeted until that is
actually true. Do not let a copywriter add them.

**The blocker no keyword fixes.** Every page renders client-side via Babel in the
browser, so there is no server-rendered HTML for a crawler to read on first
pass. Until the production rebuild ships server rendering, none of this ranks.
See `CLAUDE_CODE_HANDOFF.md` §5.
