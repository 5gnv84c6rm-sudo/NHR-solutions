One-line: the section opener — eyebrow, Title Case heading, one-or-two-sentence lead.

```jsx
<SectionHeading align="center" eyebrow="Solutions"
  title="Solutions Designed Around Your Business"
  description="Smart digital tools that cover the operational side of your company." />
```

Centre it above 3-up grids; left-align in split layouts. Wrap the highlighted words in a `<span style={{color:'var(--nhr-turquoise)'}}>` inside `title`.
